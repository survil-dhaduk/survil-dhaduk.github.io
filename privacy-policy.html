Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ScanPro ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application.

1. Permissions & Data Collection

Camera Permission

ScanPro requires camera access exclusively to detect and decode QR codes and barcodes in real-time. Camera frames are processed on-device and are never uploaded or streamed to external servers.

2. Local Data Storage

Your scan history, custom generated codes, and preferences are stored locally on your device storage. You can delete or export this history at any time through the app settings.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

To support application operations, maintenance, and advertising, we integrate the following industry-standard third-party SDKs:

  • Google AdMob: Serves banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, advertising ID, and approximate location in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies.
  • Google Firebase Analytics (GA4): Collects aggregated, non-personally identifiable diagnostic and usage events (such as screen views and feature usage) to improve application stability and user experience.

4. Children's Privacy

ScanPro does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us.

5. Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to safeguard your data. No method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, but we do not store sensitive personal information on our servers.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our developer contact on the Google Play Store / App Store listing.
